# AAA Ocean Simulation – Three.js + WebGPU + TSL

## Goal
Build the most realistic ocean simulation possible in the browser. Target: Unreal Engine / AAA cinematic quality, 60 FPS at 1440p.

Use WebGPU + TSL. Optimize aggressively for browser performance (resolutions, poly counts, texture sizes, LODs).

You are allowed (and expected) to research references and improve upon any technical approach I suggest if a better method exists.

---

## Core Requirements (Initial Spec)

Create a complete ocean experience with:

- High-quality ocean waves (prefer multi-cascade FFT or superior alternative). Support calm to choppy seas. No visible tiling.
- Seamless air ↔ water transition (including water droplets when exiting water).
- Full cinematic water shading: reflection, refraction, subsurface scattering, broadband backscatter, Beer’s law attenuation, sun glints, etc.
- Advanced shoreline simulation:
  - Waves respond correctly to decreasing depth
  - Proper rolling crests that crash
  - Realistic foam and sea spray
  - Water lapping up on beach with contact normals and shadows
  - Foam advects, fades, and leaves wetness on the sand
- Complete wake simulation integrated with shoreline
- Driveable boat with realistic physics and bow spray
- Small fishing village + pier where the boat is docked
- First-person walking on the beach → enter boat → switch between 1st and 3rd person
- Dynamic physically-based sky + volumetric clouds
- ACES filmic tonemapping
- Ambient occlusion, high-quality shadows (cascaded preferred)
- Underwater reef that can be explored (smooth walk → swim transition)
- Underwater fog and caustics
- High-quality waterline shader (glass-like edge with rounded normals)
- Persistent foam generated by wave compression
- Beautiful UI to control wave parameters, sun position, and other settings
- Show FPS counter in upper-left
- Remove HTML body margin

Identify and implement any missing features that would push this to true AAA quality.

---

## Iteration & Refinement Directives (apply progressively)

After the base system is working, continuously refine according to these priorities:

### Water & Waves
- Fix any refraction issues and flickering.
- Completely rewrite caustics if they look like noise, streaks, or have hard edges. Make them follow wave activity realistically and look AAA.
- Shoreline waves need real rolling crests with sheets of water, realistic particle spray, and foam that is pushed up the beach correctly. Foam should not roll up from the bottom of the curl.
- Sea spray must look individual and physical, not blob-like puffs. No spray rolling unrealistically off the top of waves.
- Eliminate stretched foam, bright flickering spots, and clinical/plain wave crashes. Study real-world wave breaking behavior.
- Fix gaps between shallow water simulation and lapping water when waves recede.
- Improve boat wake: better foam/aeration, less exaggerated trough, more realistic spray (reference Three.js Water Pro techniques without copying directly).
- Water surface should not flicker or smear when moving (especially inside boat).

### Terrain, Village & Details
- Massively improve terrain: better textures, tessellation, organic features, realistic trees/bushes/grass with wind, ambient occlusion, shadows, vignette.
- Add proper PBR textures + fine normal/roughness detail to the village. Remove weak/cartoony textures.
- Add debris, crates, rocks, pebbles, logs along the shoreline (realistic PBR, fine detail). Move debris ~2m further down the shore if needed.
- Improve palm tree trunks and distant trees (remove cartoony look).
- Fix tree canopies and shininess.
- Hill on the left needs trees and shrubs.
- Fix shadows under overturned boats and improve overall shadow map blending (no hard cutoffs). Use cascaded shadows.

### Underwater & Life
- Add fish, corals, plants, and a highly realistic giant whale.
- Whale needs proper textures, less shininess, more detail/bumps, better animation (especially flippers), smaller fish swimming around it, and foam churn when it breaches.
- Improve fish models.
- Add little creatures on the beach that scamper/fade when approached.
- Birds flying, diving, and making sounds.
- More underwater life overall.

### Atmosphere & Polish
- Completely overhaul volumetric clouds: broader, more epic, less height, better detail hierarchy (low-frequency bases + high-frequency tops). Fix grainy, marshmallow, or cartoony look. Improve cirrus (broad, subtle, wispy, noisy variation across the sky). Remove bad stratocumulus.
- Add atmospheric haze, god rays, bounce light, subtle motion blur, screen-space contact shadows, and very subtle floating particles in the air — without killing performance.
- Add custom AAA lens flare (not the built-in Three.js one).
- Make dock lights brighter, hanging from chains so they bobble, and cast proper light. All lights should illuminate the scene. Add controllable flashlight (works underwater).
- Let me control sun azimuth.
- Improve boat cockpit dramatically (fix z-fighting, better textures and details, better interior lighting at night). Remove boat windows if they cause issues.
- Make the fish sign sway.
- Fix any ambient occlusion artifacts (especially on fishing net and inside boat).

### Audio
- Remove synthetic/fake sounds (especially bubble and exaggerated water entry/exit).
- Make walking sounds much more subtle and realistic (prefer real free sound libraries).
- Add bird noises and 3D whale sounds.
- Restore missing audio if it disappears.

### Interaction & Polish
- Seamless air/water transition with proper clip-plane handling (no black sky, no splitting).
- Remove screen-warping distortion when exiting water.
- When switching from free camera to walking, drop the player with gravity at current position.
- Smooth swimming transition (not abrupt).
- For LODs, fade using Bayer mask instead of hard switches.
- Terrain should correctly cast shadows on water.
- Break up the shoreline so it is not one continuous long roller (more varied breaking waves).

### Performance & Finalization
- Continuously optimize. Do not let performance degrade.
- Show status of any parallel agents and their termination conditions.
- Prefer writing in TSL when possible.
- Finish remaining work efficiently once the major systems are solid.

---

## Final Expectation
Deliver a complete, polished, performant AAA-quality ocean experience in the browser that feels cinematic when zoomed out to the whole island and still holds up when examining fine details up close. Iterate until the result is excellent.
